Is Mercies free?

Yes. Daily Scripture, guided prayer, the Bible reader, highlights, notes and the Prayer Wall are free. Pro goes deeper: ask Bible Chat as much as you like, study any topic in depth, take quizzes built from your own highlights, memorize verses until they stay with you, and lead a prayer group of your own.

Are my prayers private?

Your prayers, notes and highlights are yours. Anything you post to the Prayer Wall is shared anonymously, with no name attached.

Does it work offline?

Partly. Nine Bible translations are available offline, and the BSB can be read offline after a one-time download. Saving notes and highlights, Chat, Study and the Prayer Wall need internet.

Which Bible translations are included?

Ten translations, so you can read in the one that feels like home and compare up to three side by side.

Is it on iPhone?

Mercies is on Android now, on Google Play. iPhone is coming soon.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time from inside the app, or from our delete account page.
