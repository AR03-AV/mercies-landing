Delete your account

How to remove your Mercies account in the app, what is deleted, and how to ask us for help if you cannot sign in.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Mercies and go to Preferences.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Optional reason sheet: choose why you are leaving (Not using it enough, Found another app, Subscription cost, Hard to use, or Other), add a short detail if you choose Other, then tap Continue — or tap Skip.
  4. On the confirmation sheet, read what will be deleted. If you have a paid plan, cancel it first with Manage subscription (deleting the account does not cancel App Store or Google Play billing).
  5. Tap Delete my account to confirm. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and personal data (blessings, notes, saved verses, settings, Chat and Study messages, Personalized Quiz history and account-linked quiz cache, custom highlight topics, blocks)
  • Your Prayer Wall posts and prays
  • Your group content (removed under our group cleanup rules) and your membership
  • Your push notification token
  • Your sign-in account (it cannot be recovered)

What may be kept

  • Prayer Wall reports you filed, with your account link removed (anonymized), so we can keep the community safe
  • For “Report this reply” records: the question and note are cleared; the reply text may be kept for safety review
  • Shared answer caches for common first Chat and Study questions (not linked to any account)
Deleting your Mercies account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel that separately in your device subscription settings before you delete, if you have a paid plan.

If you cannot sign in

Email help@support.merciesapp.com from the email address on your Mercies account. Ask us to delete the account and include enough detail for us to find it. We will verify the request and complete deletion within 30 days.
