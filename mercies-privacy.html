Mercies is operated by Anil F Rajayyan ("Mercies," "we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through the Mercies mobile application (the "App"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By creating an account or otherwise using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Mercies is available to users worldwide, including in the United States, Canada, the European Union and United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates, India, and Australia. Where local law gives you additional rights beyond what is described generally below, those rights are set out in the region-specific parts of Section 8 (Your Rights).

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely; we do not have access to your plaintext password). Content You Create. This includes: - Prayer requests you post to the Prayer Wall - Blessings, notes, and highlights you save - Messages you send in Bible Chat - Your responses to daily prayer prompts Prayer Wall posts are anonymous. We do not display your name, email, or any identifying information alongside a Prayer Wall post, and other users cannot see who posted a request. Because prayer requests can reveal information about your religious beliefs and practice, we treat this content with particular care — see the special-category data note in Section 8. Device Information. We collect a device identifier and, if you enable notifications, a push notification token, so we can deliver prayer reminders and blessing follow-ups to your device. Payment Information. If you subscribe to Mercies Pro, your payment is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your credit card number. We receive confirmation of your subscription status (active, cancelled, expired) from Apple/Google via RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. Usage Information. We may collect basic technical information such as app version and device type to help us fix bugs and improve the App. Mercies does not include its own crash or analytics SDK. Apple and Google may collect crash and performance data under their own terms when you use their app stores and operating systems. We do not collect: - Your precise location - Your device's contact list - Health data - Advertising identifiers for third-party ad targeting

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to: - Provide and operate the App's features (Prayer Wall, Bible reading, daily prayers, Blessings) - Send you prayer reminders and blessing follow-ups, if you've enabled notifications - Process your subscription (via Apple/Google/RevenueCat) - Respond to support requests - Review reported content, for the safety of the Prayer Wall and group communities - Improve the App and fix technical issues We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertising networks or data brokers.

Reply Service

Mercies uses a reply service provided by Anthropic to prepare automatically generated replies for: Bible Chat, Bible Study, group Study, prayers made from your own words, the Monthly Wrap, the Personalized Quiz, and highlight topic suggestions. What is sent. When you use these features after allowing the reply service, we may send what you type or share for them: your questions, your own words, blessings, notes, highlights, saved verses, studies, and the passages you study, memorize, or read. What is not sent: your name, email address, or password. Consent. This happens only after you allow it in the first-use consent sheet. You can change that anytime in Preferences under Replies and suggestions. If you say no or turn it off, nothing is sent to the reply service for these features, and Chat, Study, group Study, prayers from your own words, the Monthly Wrap, and the Personalized Quiz stay unavailable; the rest of Mercies continues to work (including mood-based prayers that do not send your free text). We do not use the content sent to the reply service to train Anthropic's models. Replies are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or incomplete. They are intended to support your own reading and reflection, not to serve as authoritative theological or pastoral guidance. Please use your own judgment, and consult a pastor, priest, or trusted spiritual advisor for matters of significant personal importance. Where required by law, we rely on your explicit consent for this processing (see Section 7).

Safety Screening

Prayer Wall posts and group posts, notes, and takeaways are screened automatically by Anthropic for safety before they appear. By posting, you accept this screening. A post may be held for review if it needs a closer look. This screening is separate from the reply-service consent above.

Report This Reply

For Bible Chat, Bible Study, and group Study, you can use Report this reply. We store the question, the reply, the reason, and any note you add, and we email them to us for review. Reporting does not require reply-service consent. When you delete your account, the question and note on those reports are cleared; the reply text may be kept for safety review.

3. Prayer Wall & Community Features

The Prayer Wall is designed to be anonymous. When you post a request: - Your name and account details are never shown to other users - Other users can pray for your request or report it if they believe it violates our guidelines - Your post is screened automatically for safety as described above, and may be held for review - If a request is reported, our moderation process (described in our Terms of Service) may remove it Blocking. If you block another user, you will no longer see their Prayer Wall posts. This does not notify the blocked user. The Prayer Wall is not a crisis response service. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis hotline in your area. Mercies is not staffed or designed to respond to emergencies in real time.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. While your account is active: - Bible Chat and Bible Study messages are kept until you delete the account, the study, or the conversation - We keep the last 15 generated Personalized Quiz sets for your account - Shared answer caches for common first Chat and Study questions are kept without an expiry. Those cache tables store only a question or topic key and the cached reply — they do not store a user id and are not linked to an account - The daily Personalized Quiz cache for your account has no separate expiry beyond account deletion You can delete your account at any time from Preferences → Delete Account. When you do: - Your personal data (blessings, notes, saved verses, settings, Chat and Study messages, Personalized Quiz history and account-linked quiz cache, custom highlight topics, blocks, and related account data) is permanently deleted - Your Prayer Wall posts and prays are deleted - Your group content is removed according to our group cleanup rules; your membership ends - Your push notification token is cleared - Prayer Wall reports you filed are kept with your account link removed (anonymized) so we can keep the community safe - For "Report this reply" records, the question and note are cleared; the reply text may be kept for safety review - Your auth account is deleted and cannot be recovered Where applicable law gives you a right to erasure, deleting the account and severing identifying links as described above is how we meet that right for content tied to your account. Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription — you must cancel that separately through your device's subscription settings.

Scripture Texts

Bible texts in Mercies are public-domain or openly licensed editions. The NET Bible is not offered. An optional Berean Standard Bible (BSB) pack may be downloaded to your device from AO Lab for offline reading. Bible Chat may briefly quote other named editions from memory; when it does, the edition is named.

6. Children's Privacy

Mercies is intended for users age 16 and older. When creating an account, you must confirm that you are at least 16 and agree to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy. We rely on that confirmation in good faith — we do not independently verify age. If we learn that someone under 16 has provided us with personal information despite this confirmation, we will delete it. A parent or guardian who believes their child has provided us with personal information can contact us at help@support.merciesapp.com to request deletion.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: - Access the personal information we hold about you - Correct inaccurate information - Delete your information (see Section 5) - Object to or restrict certain processing - Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent - Receive a copy of your data in a portable format To exercise these rights, contact us at help@support.merciesapp.com. In some jurisdictions (including the EU/UK and, for state privacy laws, parts of the US) this is treated as a special or sensitive category of personal information with extra protections. We only process this content to provide the features you use, we rely on your explicit consent where required, and you can withdraw reply-service consent anytime in Preferences under Replies and suggestions, or by deleting the relevant content or your account.
